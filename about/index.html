Helping Owners Consider a House Sale

Trusted Local Realtor has helped homeowners since 2016. Based in Spotsylvania, we serve Virginia, Washington, DC and Maryland, including Fredericksburg, Richmond, Hampton Roads and Northern Virginia.

We purchase houses directly for cash, with no repairs, cleaning, fees or commissions. We also help owners compare a cash sale with what listing through an agent could net. Our conversations cover situations such as inherited property, relocation, downsizing, major repairs and the decision to sell a rental home.

You can begin with a phone call about the property and your plans. Jot down the address, any condition concerns and the questions you'd like answered. It's fine if you're still gathering information or haven't decided to sell.

Before heading to the address below, call to confirm a suitable time. That also gives you a chance to ask what information would be useful to bring.
